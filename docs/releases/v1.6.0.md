# FUTO Voice Input 1.6.0

## Keyboard dictation screen

- Letter keys while dictating: the new ABC button opens a QWERTY keyboard.
  Shift makes the next letter capital, a second tap locks capitals. Long-press
  a letter for Slovak and Czech accents, or a top-row key for its digit
  (q is 1 through p is 0). Cursor arrows move one character. Dictation keeps
  running, and silence or a tap on the screen does not stop it while the
  letter keys are open.
- The "?" button is replaced by a "." button. It replaces trailing spaces and
  one "." or ",", so a period is never doubled.
- The editing buttons are laid out in two equal columns, with delete word and
  ABC at the right edge.

## Recognition

- When live text at pauses already shows everything you said, Stop delivers
  the text at once and the keyboard closes without waiting.
- Live text at pauses no longer stalls on a short phrase between long
  silences.
- New **Latin letters only** setting in Model Options, on by default. Parakeet
  Ultra and the Slovak model no longer write English words in Cyrillic
  letters. Turn it off if you dictate Russian, Ukrainian or Bulgarian.

## Documentation

- The model guide and user guide describe live text at pauses, the editing
  buttons and letter keys, voice commands, custom theme colors and history
  playback.
